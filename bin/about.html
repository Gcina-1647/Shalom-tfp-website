About Us

Shalom Funeral Policy was founded to provide affordable funeral cover for rural communities. Our mission is to ensure dignity and compassion during difficult times. We value honesty, accessibility, and community support.

Shalom Top Up Funeral Policy operates as a juristic representative of Structured Administration Services, an authorised financial services provider (FSP 51068). Policies are underwritten by Structured Risk Solutions, a licensed microinsurer and authorised Financial Services Provider (FSP 50618).


Vision & Values

  • Affordable funeral cover for all families
  • Transparency and trust
  • Community-driven support
