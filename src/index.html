Shalom TopUp Funeral Policy

Pay for what you can afford...

Shalom TopUp Funeral Policy is a life insurance provider specializing in funeral cover for rural communities. We focus on financially disadvantaged families who require affordable, low‑cost funeral policies. We also serve elderly and less‑educated individuals who value simple, trustworthy solutions that are easy to understand and access.

New entrants have a 6-month waiting period, with no waiting period for accidental death if the first premium is paid. Suicide carries a 12-month waiting period. Entry age ranges from 18 to 84 years, and premiums are payable monthly in advance. Claims are processed through Shalom or SAS assistance.

Funeral assistance and services

Listed services include body collection and burial arrangement assistance, a coffin, 50 funeral programmes, tent decoration, audio setup, grave-site services and obituary options. Confirm the exact benefits available under your chosen plan.

Premiums and joining fee

The homepage lists a once-off joining fee of R50 and monthly premiums of R130–R150 depending on plan and transport. Contact Shalom to confirm the exact premium before joining.

Waiting periods

New entrants have a six-month waiting period. Accidental death has no waiting period if the first premium has been paid. Suicide carries a 12-month waiting period.

Entry age and payments

The listed entry age is 18 to 84 years. Premiums are payable monthly in advance. Ask Shalom for the policy wording and full terms.

Claims assistance

The current homepage says claims are processed through Shalom or SAS assistance. Contact the office to confirm the current claims process and required documents.
