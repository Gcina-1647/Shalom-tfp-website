Shalom TopUp Funeral Policy

Pay for what you can afford...

Shalom TopUp Funeral Policy is a life insurance provider specializing in funeral cover for rural communities. We focus on financially disadvantaged families who require affordable, low‑cost funeral policies. We also serve elderly and less‑educated individuals who value simple, trustworthy solutions that are easy to understand and access.

New entrants have a 6-month waiting period, with no waiting period for accidental death if the first premium is paid. Suicide carries a 12-month waiting period. Entry age ranges from 18 to 84 years, and premiums are payable monthly in advance. Claims are processed through Shalom or SAS assistance.
