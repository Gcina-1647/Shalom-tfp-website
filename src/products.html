Our Products & Benefits

We provide comprehensive funeral cover services tailored to your needs:

  • Body collection and burial arrangement assistance
  • Provision of a coffin and 50 funeral programs
  • Tent decoration, audio setup, grave site services, obituary options.

Cover Options

  • Single Member Cover
  • Single + Children Cover
  • Family Cover
  • Extended Member Cover (children 0–17)
  • Member + 5, Member + 9, Member + 13 Cover

Premiums vary by age and cover amount. For example, a member aged 18–64 pays R39 for R5000 cover, or R159 for R20,000 cover. Children (0–17) can be covered from R25 (R5000) to R62 (R20,000).
